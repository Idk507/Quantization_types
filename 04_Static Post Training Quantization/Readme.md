, Static Post Training Quantization, PTQ, with Calibration. Here you run a small representative dataset through the model once to observe activation ranges, then fix the scale factors before deployment, using a statistical criterion such as KL divergence minimization or percentile clipping rather than raw min max to avoid outliers dominating the range. This is the standard technique for classical CNNs deployed through TensorRT, ONNX Runtime, or TensorFlow Lite.
